  <section id="family-matching">
    <h2>Family Matching Ideas</h2>
    <p>Thanksgiving is peak matching-shirt season. What works:</p>
    <ul>
      <li><strong>Coordinated color palette:</strong> fall tones — rust, mustard, forest green, cream — look great in group photos.</li>
      <li><strong>Same design, different cuts:</strong> one design across men's, women's, and kids' blanks keeps it unified without looking uniform.</li>
      <li><strong>Role shirts:</strong> "Head Chef," "Pie Expert," "Professional Napper" — fun and photo-friendly.</li>
    </ul>
    <p>Our <a href="/blog/15-adorable-matching-family-shirt-ideas-for-every-occasion-2024-guide">matching family shirt ideas guide</a> covers sizing and ordering logistics in detail, and <a href="/blog/the-ultimate-guide-to-personalized-family-reunion-shirts-design-quality-and-logistics">family reunion shirts</a> has transferable tips for big groups.</p>

  <section id="kids">
    <h2>Thanksgiving Shirts for Kids</h2>
    <ul>
      <li><strong>Cute over funny:</strong> cartoon turkeys, handprint art styles, and animal costumes (like the cat-turkey above) win with kids.</li>
      <li><strong>Soft fabric matters most:</strong> kids won't wear scratchy shirts — stick to ringspun cotton or soft blends.</li>
      <li><strong>Size up:</strong> kids grow; a slightly roomy shirt gets worn longer.</li>
    </ul>
  </section>

  <section id="fit-fabric">
    <h2>Fit & Fabric Tips</h2>
    <ul>
      <li><strong>Eat comfortably:</strong> this is the holiday of stretchy waistbands — a relaxed-fit tee beats a fitted one after dinner.</li>
      <li><strong>Layer-friendly:</strong> Thanksgiving weather varies; a tee that layers under a flannel or cardigan is the practical choice.</li>
      <li><strong>Fabric:</strong> soft cotton or tri-blends for all-day comfort. Avoid stiff prints that feel like cardboard against the skin.</li>
    </ul>

  <section id="faq">
    <h2>Frequently Asked Questions</h2>
    <h3>When should I order Thanksgiving shirts?</h3>
    <p>At least 2-3 weeks before Thanksgiving. Fall is peak season for print-on-demand, so production queues run longer than usual.</p>
    <h3>Are matching family Thanksgiving shirts still popular?</h3>
    <p>Yes — coordinated family outfits remain one of the most popular holiday trends, especially for the group photo.</p>
    <h3>What's the funniest Thanksgiving shirt style?</h3>
    <p>Food-coma humor and turkey puns are perennial winners. Meme-based designs are fun but date quickly.</p>
    <h3>What should kids wear for Thanksgiving?</h3>
    <p>Something cute, soft, and slightly roomy — they'll be more comfortable, and the photos turn out better.</p>
