<h2>Buying Advice: Getting the Details Right</h2>
<h3>Sizing</h3>
<ul>
<li><strong>Women's fitted vs. unisex:</strong> if she wears fitted tops, don't hand her a boxy unisex tee. Check what she owns — mirror that cut.</li>
<li><strong>When guessing:</strong> between sizes, size up. A slightly relaxed tee is worn; a too-tight one isn't.</li>
<li><strong>Sweatshirts:</strong> most moms prefer them roomy. One size up from her tee size is the safe call.</li>
</ul>
<h3>Fabric</h3>
<ul>
<li><strong>Combed ringspun cotton</strong> or tri-blends are the comfort sweet spot — softer and longer-lasting than basic cotton.</li>
<li><strong>For new moms:</strong> organic cotton is worth it for sensitive, newborn-close wear.</li>
<li><strong>For everyday wear:</strong> tri-blends (cotton/poly/rayon) resist shrinking and keep their drape wash after wash.</li>
</ul>
<h3>Personalization &amp; printing</h3>
<ul>
<li><strong>Double-check names, dates, and spelling</strong> before ordering anything personalized — personalized items usually can't be returned.</li>
<li><strong>Screen printing</strong> lasts longest; <strong>DTG</strong> is best for detailed, colorful designs; vinyl transfers can crack with heavy wear.</li>
<li><strong>Order early</strong> for Mother's Day — personalized items need 1–2 extra weeks of lead time.</li>
</ul>

<h2>FAQ: World's Best Mom Gifts</h2>
<h3>What's the best gift for a new mom?</h3>
<p>Practical comfort wins: soft-wash tees or sweatshirts in skin-safe fabrics, darker colors, and a relaxed fit. Pair with real help — a meal, a nap covered by someone else — and you've nailed it.</p>
<h3>How far in advance should I order a personalized mom shirt?</h3>
<p>At least 2–3 weeks before Mother's Day or her birthday. Personalized items take longer to produce and usually can't be returned, so leave buffer for corrections.</p>
<h3>Is a "World's Best Mom" shirt too cheesy?</h3>
<p>It depends on the mom. The classic script version reads heartfelt; the funny versions ("Just Ask My Kids," "World's Okayest Mom") read knowing. Match the design to her humor, not yours.</p>
<h3>What if I don't know her size?</h3>
<p>Check her closet for a well-loved tee and match the brand's size chart — or ask someone close to her. Between sizes, size up; a relaxed fit gets worn, a tight one doesn't.</p>
