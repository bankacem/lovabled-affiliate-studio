  <section id="couples">
    <h2>Matching Shirts for Couples</h2>
    <p>The classic move. What works without looking costume-y:</p>
    <ul>
      <li><strong>Coordinated, not identical:</strong> same color family and theme, with complementary designs ("His Queen" / "Her King" is dated — try shared graphics in different cuts instead).</li>
      <li><strong>Subtle over loud:</strong> a small embroidered-look heart or minimalist line-art couple reads romantic; giant photo prints read novelty.</li>
      <li><strong>Wearable after:</strong> the best couple shirts are ones you'd each wear alone. If it only makes sense as a pair, it's a one-day shirt.</li>
    </ul>
    <p>For more pairing ideas, see our <a href="/blog/matching-couple-shirts-the-ultimate-2024-style-guide-for-lovebirds">couple shirts style guide</a> and <a href="/blog/funny-couple-shirts-101-hilarious-matching-designs-for-couples-with-a-sense-of-humor">101 funny couple shirts</a> if humor is your love language.</p>

  <section id="singles">
    <h2>Shirts for Singles</h2>
    <p>Single on Valentine's Day is a design genre of its own — and honestly some of the funniest shirts in the category:</p>
    <ul>
      <li><strong>Anti-Valentine's humor:</strong> "Cupid's target practice," "Single and thriving," broken-heart graphics with a wink.</li>
      <li><strong>Self-love statements:</strong> "My own Valentine" energy — confident, not bitter.</li>
      <li><strong>Keep it wearable:</strong> the joke should land in March too, or it's a drawer shirt.</li>
    </ul>

  <section id="friends">
    <h2>Friend Group & Galentine's Ideas</h2>
    <p>Galentine's (Feb 13) has become as big as the main event for friend groups:</p>
    <ul>
      <li><strong>Matching group tees:</strong> same design, everyone's size — brunch photos basically take themselves.</li>
      <li><strong>Role-based sets:</strong> "Brunch Captain," "Mimosa Expert" — fun without being couple-exclusive.</li>
      <li><strong>Order timing:</strong> print-on-demand takes 5-10 business days, so order at least 2 weeks before Feb 14.</li>
    </ul>
  </section>

  <section id="fit-fabric">
    <h2>Fit & Fabric Tips</h2>
    <ul>
      <li><strong>For photos:</strong> solid colors (dusty pink, cream, black) photograph better than busy patterns.</li>
      <li><strong>Fabric:</strong> tri-blends and ringspun cotton feel soft and drape well — important if the shirt gets worn beyond the holiday.</li>
      <li><strong>His-and-hers cuts:</strong> if ordering a pair, don't default to two unisex blanks — a fitted women's cut and a classic men's cut look better together.</li>
    </ul>
  </section>

  <section id="faq">
    <h2>Frequently Asked Questions</h2>
    <h3>When should I order Valentine's Day shirts?</h3>
    <p>At least 2 weeks before February 14. Print-on-demand production plus shipping typically takes 5-10 business days.</p>
    <h3>Are matching couple shirts cringey?</h3>
    <p>Only the lazy ones. Coordinated designs in good colors look intentional; identical giant photo prints look like a costume.</p>
    <h3>What should singles wear on Valentine's Day?</h3>
    <p>Whatever you want — but the anti-Valentine's humor category exists for a reason and makes great party conversation.</p>
    <h3>Do Valentine's shirts work as gifts?</h3>
    <p>Yes, especially niche ones — like the nurse-themed design above — that show you thought about the person, not just the holiday.</p>
