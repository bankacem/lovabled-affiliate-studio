    <h2>Summer Outfit Ideas with Graphic Tees</h2>
    <p>Now that we've established the awesomeness of graphic tees, let's dive into some summer outfit ideas that feature them as the main attraction. Here are a few of our favorites:</p>
    <ul>

        <li>Wear a graphic tee with a flowy skirt and sandals for a bohemian-inspired outfit.</li>

<section id="tips-and-tricks">
    <h2>Tips and Tricks for Styling Graphic Tees</h2>
    <p>Now that you've chosen the perfect graphic tee, it's time to style it. Here are some tips and tricks to help you create a stylish and comfortable outfit:</p>
    <ul>
        <li>Balance bold graphics with neutral-colored bottoms.</li>
        <li>Layer a graphic tee under a cardigan or jacket for a more polished look.</li>
        <li>Pair a graphic tee with a statement piece of jewelry, such as a bold necklace or earrings.</li>
        <li>Experiment with different fabrics, such as cotton, polyester, or blends.</li>
    </ul>
    <p>Remember, the key to styling graphic tees is to have fun and experiment with different combinations. Don't be afraid to try new things and step out of your comfort zone.</p>
