Mugs created using dye sublimation are generally dishwasher safe because the ink is embedded into the coating. However, mugs with vinyl decals or screen printing should always be hand-washed to prevent peeling or fading.</p><h3>What is the standard size for a personalized mug?</h3><p>The industry standard size is 11 ounces. However, 15-ounce mugs (often called "jumbo" or "bistro" mugs) are becoming increasingly popular for those who prefer a larger serving of coffee or tea.</p><h3>Can I put a personalized stainless steel mug in the microwave?</h3><p>No, absolutely not. Stainless steel and metal enamel mugs should never be placed in a microwave as they will spark and can cause a fire or damage your appliance.</p><h3>How long does it take to print a custom mug?</h3><p>Production times vary by vendor. Sublimation printing is quick and can often be done in 1-3 business days. Bulk screen printing orders may take 1-2 weeks. Always check the shipping and processing times if ordering for a specific holiday.</p>
