  <section id="for-her">
    <h2>Best 40th Birthday Shirts for Her</h2>
    <p>The "Birthday Queen" and "Forty and Fabulous" styles are popular for a reason — they're celebratory without being tacky, and the best versions use soft, fitted blanks that actually flatter.</p>

    <h3>What to look for</h3>
    <ul>
      <li><strong>Soft-hand prints:</strong> water-based or DTG inks that sink into the fabric instead of sitting on top like plastic</li>
      <li><strong>Fitted or relaxed women's cuts:</strong> avoid boxy unisex blanks if she prefers a shaped fit</li>
      <li><strong>Script typography:</strong> elegant cursive "Fabulous" lettering photographs beautifully</li>
    </ul>
    <p>A practical tip: the minimalist left-chest "Forty" in serif type tends to get worn long after the party, while big bold joke prints are usually one-night-only. If you're also planning <a href="/blog/bachelorette-party-shirt-ideas-2026-the-ultimate-guide-to-trends-fabrics-and-custom-designs">bachelorette party shirts</a>, the same fitted-blank rule applies.</p>
  </section>

  <section id="for-him">
    <h2>Best 40th Birthday Shirts for Him</h2>
    <p>For men, the reliable winners are vintage birth-year designs: "Vintage 1985 — Aged to Perfection" and "Legend Since 1985." They hit the sweet spot of funny, masculine, and genuinely wearable.</p>

    <h3>What to look for</h3>
    <ul>
      <li><strong>Distressed textures:</strong> a weathered, cracked-ink effect reads as authentic vintage; solid white block print reads as cheap</li>
      <li><strong>Heavier cotton or tri-blends:</strong> a substantial feel — 6oz+ cotton or a quality tri-blend</li>
      <li><strong>Badge typography:</strong> the whiskey-label badge layout is a classic for milestone birthdays</li>
    </ul>
    <p>Skip the "Lordy Lordy Look Who's 40" unless the birthday guy specifically loves dad jokes — the vintage-badass angle gets worn far more often.</p>

  <section id="group">
    <h2>Group & Matching Squad Shirts</h2>
    <p>Coordinating a crew? Here's what works — and what to avoid:</p>
    <ul>
      <li><strong>Coordinated beats identical:</strong> same color palette and theme, but let the birthday star's shirt stand out (gold print while the crew wears white, for example)</li>
      <li><strong>Order 2 weeks early:</strong> print-on-demand turnaround is usually 5-10 business days; rush shipping exists but costs more</li>
      <li><strong>Collect sizes upfront:</strong> use a simple shared spreadsheet — chasing sizes last-minute is the #1 group-order headache</li>
      <li><strong>Fun role shirts:</strong> "Birthday Squad," "40 & Fabulous Crew," or personalized roles ("Chief Shot Officer") make great photos</li>
    </ul>

  <section id="fabrics">
    <h2>Fabric Guide: What Feels Good</h2>
    <p>A beautiful design on a scratchy shirt is money wasted. Here's the quick fabric cheat sheet:</p>
    <ul>
      <li><strong>Tri-blends (poly/cotton/rayon):</strong> the gold standard — soft, drapes well, vintage feel from day one</li>
      <li><strong>Ringspun cotton:</strong> smoother and softer than basic carded cotton; a safe premium pick</li>
      <li><strong>CVC (60/40 cotton-poly):</strong> durable with a slight heathered look; great for group orders</li>
      <li><strong>Avoid:</strong> stiff heavyweight "boxy" blanks unless the streetwear oversized look is the goal</li>
    </ul>
    <p>Printing method matters too: DTG (direct-to-garment) and DTF (direct-to-film) both beat old-school plastisol for softness on small custom runs.</p>

  <section id="faq">
    <h2>Frequently Asked Questions</h2>
    <h3>How far in advance should I order custom 40th birthday shirts?</h3>
    <p>Order at least 2 weeks before the party. Print-on-demand production plus shipping typically takes 5-10 business days, and you'll want buffer for size exchanges.</p>
    <h3>What's the most popular 40th birthday shirt style?</h3>
    <p>Vintage birth-year designs ("Aged to Perfection", "Vintage 1985") are perennial favorites across marketplaces — they work for men and women and suit almost any party vibe.</p>
    <h3>Are matching group shirts tacky?</h3>
    <p>Not if you do them right. The modern approach is coordinated rather than identical: same theme and colors, with the birthday person's design elevated. Done well, they make the best party photos.</p>
    <h3>What size should I order in print-on-demand shirts?</h3>
    <p>Check the specific blank's size chart — they vary by brand. When in doubt between sizes, sizing up usually gives a more comfortable relaxed fit.</p>
