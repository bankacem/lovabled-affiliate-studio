    <p>Whether you're a fan of bold graphics, witty slogans, or simple designs, there's a graphic tee out there for you. And with the rise of affordable fashion, you don't have to sacrifice style for savings. So, what makes a great graphic tee? Let's dive in and find out.</p>

  <section id="what-to-look-for">
    <h2>What to Look for in a Great Graphic Tee</h2>
    <p>When shopping for a graphic tee, there are a few key things to consider. First and foremost, think about the material. Do you prefer soft, breathable cotton or a more durable blend? Consider the fit, too - do you like your tees loose and relaxed or fitted and slim? And of course, there's the design itself. What kind of graphics or slogans do you like? Are you into bold and bright, or more subdued and minimalist?</p>
    <p>Another important factor is the quality of the print. A good graphic tee should have a crisp, clear design that won't fade or crack after a few washes. Look for tees with high-quality inks and printing methods, such as screen printing or direct-to-garment printing. And don't forget to check the care instructions - some graphic tees may require special washing or drying to keep them looking their best.</p>
