  <section id="personalization">
    <h2>Personalization Options</h2>
    <p>"Personalized" means different things on different sites. Here's the honest breakdown of what's actually available:</p>
    <ul>
      <li><strong>Name personalization:</strong> designs with a customizable name field ("Luna's Mom"). Common on Etsy; rare on print-on-demand marketplaces, which sell fixed designs.</li>
      <li><strong>Photo portraits:</strong> your cat's photo turned into illustrated artwork. This is a custom-order service (Etsy sellers do this well) — not something you get from a standard marketplace listing. Order early; custom work takes longer.</li>
      <li><strong>Breed-specific designs:</strong> Siamese, Maine Coon, tabby, black cat — picking her cat's actual breed is the easiest "personalization" that works on any marketplace. Our featured designs above include a black-cat pick for exactly this reason.</li>
      <li><strong>Memorial pieces:</strong> for a cat who's passed, a portrait-style design on quality fabric becomes a keepsake. If the shirt honors a cat who's crossed the rainbow bridge, our <a href="/blog/honoring-a-lifetime-of-loyalty-the-ultimate-guide-to-personalized-pet-memorial-gifts">pet memorial gifts guide</a> handles that with extra care.</li>
    </ul>
    <p>Rule of thumb: if you need it by a specific date, buy a fixed design from a marketplace. If you want her cat's actual face on the shirt, start the custom order at least a month ahead.</p>

    <h3>Photo Tips for Custom Portraits</h3>
    <p>Custom portrait sellers work from the photo you send, so the photo is the product. What gets the best result:</p>
    <ul>
      <li><strong>Eye-level, front-facing:</strong> get down to the cat's height and shoot straight at the face. Top-down phone shots flatten features.</li>
      <li><strong>Natural light:</strong> near a window, no flash. Flash blows out fur detail and turns eyes green.</li>
      <li><strong>High resolution:</strong> send the original file, not a screenshot of a screenshot. Blurry photos print blurry.</li>
      <li><strong>Clean background:</strong> a plain wall or bedspread makes it easier for the artist to isolate the cat.</li>
    </ul>
  </section>

  <section id="design-styles">
    <h2>Design Styles That Work</h2>
    <p>Cat mom shirts fall into a few camps. Knowing which one she is makes choosing easy:</p>
    <ul>
      <li><strong>Funny / meme:</strong> sarcastic quotes, grumpy cats, relatable chaos. The safest bet for gifts — humor is universal.</li>
      <li><strong>Cute / aesthetic:</strong> pastel illustrations, kawaii faces, floral cats. Popular with younger buyers and great for everyday wear.</li>
      <li><strong>Niche hobby crossover:</strong> cat + baking, cat + books, cat + gardening. These feel personal because they reference her actual life.</li>
      <li><strong>Minimalist:</strong> small chest print, line-art cat, subtle text. The one most likely to be worn weekly rather than saved for laundry day.</li>
    </ul>
    <p>If you're buying for a holiday, our <a href="/blog/cat-lover-gifts-2026-77-purr-fect-ideas-for-feline-fans">cat lover gifts guide</a> has 77 ideas beyond shirts.</p>

    <h3>Match the Design to the Cat</h3>
    <p>The fastest way to make a mass-produced shirt feel personal: match the artwork to her actual cat. Black cat? Pick a black-cat design like our ramen tee above. Orange tabby?   <section id="fit-guide">
    <h2>Fit Guide: Finding Your Cut</h2>
    <p>Fit is where most cat mom shirt purchases go wrong. Quick rules:</p>
    <ul>
      <li><strong>Relaxed / classic fit:</strong> the default — comfortable, true to size, works for most people.</li>
      <li><strong>Fitted women's cut:</strong> shaped through the waist; size up if between sizes.</li>
      <li><strong>Oversized:</strong> trendy and cozy, but check the size chart — "oversized" blanks vary wildly by brand.</li>
    </ul>

  <section id="fabric-guide">
    <h2>Fabric &amp; Print Guide</h2>
    <p>Two things determine whether the shirt gets worn or shelved:</p>
    <ul>
      <li><strong>Fabric:</strong> ringspun cotton or tri-blends feel soft from day one. Basic heavyweight cotton is durable but can feel stiff — fine for workwear, less fun for lounging.</li>
      <li><strong>Print method:</strong> DTG (direct-to-garment) gives soft, detailed prints ideal for illustrated cat designs. Screen printing is bolder and longer-lasting for simple graphics. Embroidery is premium and permanent — a stitched cat never cracks or peels, and suits minimalist designs best.</li>
    </ul>
  </section>

  <section id="care-tips">
    <h2>Care Tips: Keeping Prints Fresh</h2>
    <ol>
      <li><strong>Wash inside out:</strong> protects the graphic from abrasion in the machine.</li>
      <li><strong>Cold water only:</strong> heat is the number one cause of print cracking.</li>
      <li><strong>Air dry when you can:</strong> the dryer is the enemy of graphic tees; low heat if you must.</li>
    </ol>
    <p>Follow these three steps and a quality cat mom tee will look new for years, not months.</p>
  </section>

  <section id="gift-playbook">
    <h2>Gift-Buying Playbook</h2>
    <p>Buying for a cat mom? Follow this order of operations:</p>
    <ol>
      <li><strong>Identify her lane:</strong> funny, cute, minimalist, or hobby-crossover (see design styles above). When in doubt, funny is the safest.</li>
      <li><strong>Match the cat:</strong> black cat? Tabby? Pick the design that looks like her actual cat — it reads as personal even on a mass-produced shirt.</li>
      <li><strong>Size up, not down:</strong> if you're unsure of her size, go one up. An oversized cat mom tee gets worn; a tight one gets regifted.</li>
      <li><strong>Check the blank:</strong> Comfort Colors and Bella+Canvas blanks are the crowd-pleasers. Avoid no-name thin blanks for gifts.</li>
      <li><strong>Order early:</strong> two weeks minimum for marketplace shipping; a month for anything custom.</li>
      <li><strong>Skip the joke if unsure:</strong> a sarcastic "crazy cat lady" shirt is funny to some and insulting to others. Cute beats edgy for gifts.</li>
    </ol>
  </section>

  <section id="faq">
    <h2>Frequently Asked Questions</h2>
    <h3>What size should I order?</h3>
    <p>Check the specific blank's size chart — they vary by brand. When between sizes on a fitted women's cut, sizing up is usually the safer choice.</p>
    <h3>Will the print crack after washing?</h3>
    <p>Quality DTG and screen prints hold up well if you wash inside-out on cold and skip the dryer. Cheap plastisol prints on thin blanks are the ones that crack early.</p>
    <h3>Are cat mom shirts a good gift?</h3>
    <p>Yes — they're personal without being risky. Pick a design that matches her humor (funny vs. cute) and you can't go far wrong.</p>
    <h3>Can I get a shirt with my own cat's photo?</h3>
    <p>Etsy sellers offer custom pet-portrait shirts; marketplaces like Redbubble sell pre-made designs only. Custom takes longer, so order early.</p>
    <h3>What's the difference between a cat mom shirt and a cat lover shirt?</h3>
    <p>Mostly the wording on the design. "Cat mom" shirts lean into the parenting identity (often with humor about it); "cat lover" shirts are broader. Buy whichever matches how she describes herself.</p>
