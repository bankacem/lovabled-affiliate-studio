Your bottom line will thank you.</p><h2>Frequently Asked Questions</h2><h3>What is a Minimum Order Quantity (MOQ)?</h3><p>MOQ stands for Minimum Order Quantity. It is the lowest number of units a supplier is willing to sell in a single order. This ensures the manufacturer covers their setup costs and makes the production run profitable.</p><h3>Is it better to buy wholesale or factory direct?</h3><p>It depends on your volume. Wholesale is great for smaller businesses (50-100 units) needing faster turnaround. Factory direct offers the lowest price but requires high volume (500+ units) and longer lead times.</p><h3>How do I calculate the landed cost of a bulk order?</h3><p>Landed cost includes the unit price plus shipping, insurance, customs duties, and taxes. Divide this total sum by the number of units to get your true cost per item. Never base your margins solely on the factory sticker price!</p><h3>Can I return a bulk order if it doesn't sell?</h3><p>Generally, no. Unlike consumer retail, B2B transactions rarely offer returns for unsold inventory. You can usually only return items if they are defective or if they do not match the approved sample you signed off on.</p><hr><p><strong>Conclusion:</strong> Scaling <a href="/blog/the-ultimate-guide-to-print-on-demand-in-2025-start-your-business-today" class="auto-link internal-link" title="The Ultimate Guide to Print on Demand in 2025: Start Your Business Today">your business</a> is a journey, not a sprint.
