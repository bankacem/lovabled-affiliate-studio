<p>There are few moments in life as exciting as finding out whether you are welcoming a little prince or a princess into your family. The "Beer &amp; Diapers" Exchange</h3><p>A popular shirt for Dad that reads <strong>"Drinking for Two"</strong> (with a beer graphic) paired with Mom’s shirt that reads <strong>"Eating for Two."</strong></p><hr><h2>Category 2: Family &amp; Sibling Focused</h2><p>Getting the siblings involved is crucial to preventing jealousy and building excitement.
