If you must use a dryer, use the lowest heat setting possible.</p></li><li><p><strong>Never Iron the Print:</strong> Direct heat from an iron will melt vinyl and damage screen prints. If you are ordering a custom or handmade item from platforms like Etsy, aim for late October.</p><h3>Are Thanksgiving shirts usually true to size?</h3><p>Most Thanksgiving shirts are printed on standard "Unisex" blanks (like Bella+Canvas or Gildan). These usually run true to size for men but may feel slightly oversized for women. Always check the specific brand's size chart before purchasing.</p><h3>What are the most popular colors for holiday shirts?</h3><p>The most popular colors reflect the autumn season: Burnt Orange, Maroon/Burgundy, Mustard Yellow, Forest Green, and Charcoal Gray.</p><h3>Can I wear a Thanksgiving shirt to a formal dinner?</h3><p>While <a target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer nofollow" class="text-primary underline underline-offset-4 hover:text-primary/80 auto-link internal-link" href="/blog/the-ultimate-style-guide-10-fresh-ways-to-wear-graphic-tees">graphic tees</a> are generally casual, you can "dress up" a minimalist Thanksgiving shirt by layering it under a blazer or tucking it into a high-quality skirt or trousers. However, for strictly formal events, a themed button-down or blouse might be more appropriate.</p><h3>How do I prevent the design from cracking?</h3><p>Cracking is usually caused by heat. Avoid hot water washes and high-heat drying. Washing the garment inside out also reduces the friction that leads to wear and tear on the design.</p><h3>Are there Thanksgiving shirts for pets?</h3><p>Yes! Many retailers now offer matching "human and dog" shirt sets. Alternatively, Thanksgiving-themed bandanas are a popular and comfortable choice for pets who don't like wearing full shirts.</p>
