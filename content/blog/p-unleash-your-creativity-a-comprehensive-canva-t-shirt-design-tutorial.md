<section id="getting-started">
  <h2>Getting Started with Canva</h2>
  <p>Canva is an incredible design platform that has taken the world by storm. With its user-friendly interface and plethora of design tools, it's no wonder why designers and non-designers alike are flocking to this platform. To get started, simply sign up for a free account on Canva's website and explore the various design options available.</p>
  <p>One of the best things about Canva is its vast template library. With thousands of pre-designed templates to choose from, you can easily find a template that suits your needs. For t-shirt design, Canva offers a wide range of templates that cater to different styles, from simple and minimalist to complex and bold.</p>
</section>
<section id="designing-your-t-shirt">
  <h2>Designing Your T-Shirt: A Step-by-Step Guide</h2>
  <p>Now that you've signed up for Canva and explored the template library, it's time to start designing your t-shirt. Here's a step-by-step guide to help you get started:</p>
  <ol>
    <li>Choose a template: Browse through Canva's t-shirt template library and select a template that resonates with your design idea.</li>
    <li>Customize the template: Use Canva's design tools to customize the template. You can change the font, color, and layout to suit your needs.</li>
    <li>Add graphics and images: Canva offers a vast library of graphics and images that you can use to enhance your design. You can also upload your own images or graphics.</li>
    <li>Experiment with text: Play around with different font styles, sizes, and colors to create a unique and eye-catching design.</li>
    <li>Download and print: Once you're happy with your design, download it as a PDF or PNG file and print it on your t-shirt.</li>
  </ol>
  <p>Remember, the key to creating a stunning t-shirt design is to keep it simple, yet bold. Don't be afraid to experiment and try out new things – after all, that's what design is all about!</p>
</section>
