In 2026, it falls on June 16th.</p><h3>What are the most popular Father's Day gift categories?</h3><p>The top categories typically include electronics, tools/home improvement gear, outdoor/sporting goods, and personal care items. However, "Special Outings" (restaurants/events) usually see the highest total spending.</p><h3>How much should I spend on a Father's Day gift?</h3><p>While the average spend is around $170, there is no set rule. Most fathers value the thought and utility of a gift over its price tag. A $20 tool he uses every day is often better than a $200 gadget that sits in a drawer.</p><h3>What is a good gift for a "new" dad?</h3><p>For first-time fathers, sentimental gifts like "Dad and Me" matching outfits, a high-quality camera to capture memories, or even a "diaper bag" that looks like a rugged tactical backpack are popular choices.</p><h3>Are gift cards considered a bad Father's Day gift?</h3><p>Not at all! Many dads prefer the flexibility of choosing their own specific tool or tech component. To make it feel more personal, pair the gift card with a small physical item related to the store (e.g., a bag of coffee with a Starbucks card).</p><h3>What if my dad says he doesn't want anything?</h3><p>In this case, focus on "consumable" gifts or experiences. High-end steaks, a bottle of his favorite spirit, or simply spending the day together doing an activity he enjoys are ways to honor him without cluttering his space.</p>
